Progress in this field is often set by what can be measured rather than by what can be imagined.
Motivated by these limits, I develop ultrafast optical and diffraction instrumentation—spanning
nonlinear spectroscopies, ultrafast electron diffraction, and time-resolved X-ray scattering—to
extend the sensitivity, speed, and range of conditions over which the dynamics of quantum
materials can be observed.

K. A. Morey, B. T. Fichera, B. Lv, **Z. Shen**, N. Gedik, "Automated polarization rotation for multi-axis rotational-anisotropy second harmonic generation experiments", [Review of Scientific Instruments 96, 043002 (2025)](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/rsi/article/96/4/043002/3342451/Automated-polarization-rotation-for-multi-axis).
